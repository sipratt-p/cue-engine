# Draft email to JPOD

To: admin@jpodtbc.com
Subject: Your mixes with song-level skipping (small app, no strings)

Hi JPOD,

I'm a longtime listener of the BlissCoast series. Dubs & Daydreams has songs I'd put on repeat and one or two I'd skip every time, which in a 60-minute mix means scrubbing blind in the car.

So I built a small iPhone player called Cue. You add a mix, it finds where each song starts, and Next on the steering wheel jumps to the next song. I ran all 17 of your published mixes through it using the tracklists on jpodtbc.com, and they work.

Two things I'd love from you, both optional:

1. Permission to include one or two of your mixes as the built-in demo, with credit and a link back to the download. They're free on your site, but that isn't a licence, so I'm asking.
2. If you ever want an official cue sheet, exact starts for each song as you'd mark them, the app can carry yours instead of its guesses.

Nothing about your distribution changes. Cue plays files people already have and never hosts or streams audio.

Here's a 30-second video of it running on Dubs & Daydreams: https://github.com/sipratt-p/cue-engine/blob/main/docs/media/demo.mp4
The engine that finds the song starts is open source: https://github.com/sipratt-p/cue-engine

Thanks for the music,
Seth

# For DJs

**Your mixes, with every song reachable.**

Cue is a mixtape player. Listeners add your mix and get song-by-song skipping, lock-screen titles, and car controls, without leaving the mix format you made.

**Claim your mixes.** Publish an official cue sheet: the exact start of every song, named the way you name them. Your listeners get it instantly; nobody else's guesses override yours.

**Nothing changes about how you distribute.** Cue plays files people already have. It doesn't host, stream, or redistribute your audio, and it doesn't touch your SoundCloud or Mixcloud stats.

**Your tracklist becomes searchable.** "Which JPOD mix has Chase The Devil in it" is a question your fans ask. Cue answers it.

**How to publish a cue sheet**

1. Paste your timestamped tracklist, or let Cue detect the starts and correct the few it gets wrong.
2. Attach it to your mix page. Listeners who add that mix get your version.
